Biryani

Description

This is a simple biryani recipe.

Who doesn't love a good biryani? As a Biryani lover, I can say that this is one of the best dishes to have on a special occasion. The aroma of the spices and the taste of the rice and meat is just heavenly. This recipe is simple and easy to follow, so you can make it at home without any hassle. Gotta love it!!

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, bone-in pieces
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1-2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 cup basmati rice

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, red chili powder, turmeric, biryani masala, salt, and lemon juice. Let it sit for at least 30 minutes (overnight is better).
  2. Soak the basmati rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain.
  3. Boil water with whole spices (bay leaves, cardamoms, cloves, cinnamon, cumin seeds) and salt. Add the soaked rice and par-boil until it's about 70% cooked. Drain and set aside.
  4. In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat oil and cook the marinated chicken until it's about 80% done and the gravy has thickened slightly.
  5. Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken. Repeat with another layer of chicken and rice if needed.
  6. Top with fried onions, chopped coriander, mint leaves, and the saffron milk drizzled evenly across.
  7. Cover the pot tightly (seal with dough if doing the traditional method) and cook on low heat (dum) for 20-25 minutes.
  8. Let it rest for 5-10 minutes before opening. Gently mix the layers before serving.
