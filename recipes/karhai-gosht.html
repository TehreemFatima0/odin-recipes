Karhai Gosht

Description

This is a simple karhai gosht recipe.

Who doesn't crave a yummy karhai gosht? As a Karhai Gosht lover, I can say that this is also one of the best dishes to have on a special occasion. The aroma of the spices and the taste of the meat is just heavenly. This recipe is simple and easy to follow, so you can make it at home without any hassle. Gotta love it!!

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mutton or beef, bone-in, cut into pieces
  • 6 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tbsp ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 6 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 150g plain yogurt
  • 2 tsp black pepper, freshly cracked
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • Fresh ginger and cilantro, for garnish
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pan or karahi on high heat. Add the mutton pieces and sear until browned on all sides, about 10 minutes.
  2. Add the ginger paste and garlic paste, and cook for 2 minutes, stirring well.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook on high heat until they break down completely and the oil starts to separate, about 20 minutes, stirring frequently.
  4. Lower the heat and add the yogurt, stirring continuously to prevent it from curdling.
  5. Add the black pepper, cumin seeds, coriander powder, and red chili powder. Cook until the sauce thickens and the oil floats to the top.
  6. Stir in the garam masala, then top with the sliced green chilies, ginger julienne, and fresh cilantro.
  7. Serve hot in the karahi with naan or roti.
